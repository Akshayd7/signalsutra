What is this? Shows candle-pattern zones and price levels where price previously reacted, plus current strength rankings for the top 30 Binance coins. Green = recent strength, red = recent weakness; the number is a heuristic momentum and structure score (0-100), not a probability.

How to use it: tap any coin to open its mechanical plan: an entry zone, a stop level, and nearby price levels (Targets, Resistance above, Support below) taken from recent candles.

Zones come from closed candles only: fresh (untouched), retested (touched, still valid), or dropped once a candle closes through them.

What do these words mean?
OB (order block)
A candle-pattern zone inferred from the last opposite candle before a strong move. Price sometimes reacts when it returns, with no guarantee. Used here as the Entry zone.
Liquidity
Price levels where the market reacted before (equal highs/lows, swing points). Price often revisits such levels, but there is no guarantee.
Equal highs / lows
Two or more highs (or lows) at nearly the same price. Used here as a reference level.
Sweep
Price briefly trades past a level and then closes back inside it.
BOS
Break of structure: price closed beyond a recent high or low.
Stop
A level beyond the entry zone where this pattern no longer holds.
Resistance / Support
Reference levels above / below price where price previously reacted.
How it works, and what it can't see
Method
Uses Binance spot candles only (5m, 15m or 1h, last 200). Zones, swing points and equal highs/lows are inferred from open/high/low/close with fixed rules. Structure uses closed candles; the forming candle is used only for current price.
Zone states
One rule everywhere, from closed candles only. Fresh: no closed candle has overlapped the zone since the move. Retested: a closed candle has, and the zone is still valid. Dropped: a candle closed beyond its far edge. The live candle never changes the state. If live price is already beyond the far edge, the board says "price through zone" and the Position Builder hides it until the candle closes.
Order block (OB)
The last opposite-colour candle before a strong candle (body over 1.3x ATR).
Levels
Swing highs/lows, plus equal highs/lows (clusters within 0.12%). Levels that price has since wicked through are not shown as unswept. A sweep needs a wick past the cluster extreme and a close back inside.
BOS tag
Shown when the strong candle closed beyond the most recent swing point that had not already been broken.
Cannot see
Order book, open interest, funding, futures, news, or who is trading. It does not know of any real orders. Levels are patterns in past prices only.
