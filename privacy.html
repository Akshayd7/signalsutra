Privacy policy

SignalSutra Guide · last updated 2026-11-07

SignalSutra Guide is an educational tool that shows candle-pattern zones and past price levels for popular crypto pairs. It is not financial advice and it does not place trades.

In short: no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking. Your settings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings and alert history are saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage) on this device only. They are never sent to us.
  • The app's own files are cached by a service worker so it can open offline. You can clear both at any time from your browser or app settings ("Clear storage" / "Clear data").

Market data from Binance

Your browser downloads public market data (prices and candles) directly from Binance's public endpoints, for example data-api.binance.vision and api.binance.com, plus Binance's public market-data stream for live volume. No key or account is used. Binance receives these requests the way any website receives a visit (for example your IP address and browser type) under its own privacy policy. SignalSutra Guide is not affiliated with or endorsed by Binance.

Optional AI quick check

Nothing is sent unless you tap AI quick check on a zone card. When you do, the app sends the values of that selected zone to the owner's Cloudflare Worker (signalsutra-ai-quick-check.pkt3f1.workers.dev): the coin symbol, long/short side, zone state, zone low and high, price, zone age in candles, timeframe, and the time you tapped. The worker returns a short AI-written read. No name, email, account or device identifier is included. Cloudflare processes the request under its own privacy policy. The result is stored only in your browser's local storage.

What we do not do

  • No sign-in or user accounts.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party fonts or scripts.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone.
